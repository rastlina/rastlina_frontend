## Care and packaging evidence

The public articles link RHS houseplant guidance, University of Minnesota Extension's indoor-pest guidance, GrowTropicals' arrival-care guide, The Sill's repotting guidance, Bloomscape's unpacking guidance and FedEx's general live-plant packaging guidance. These support checking moisture before watering, providing appropriate light, observing new plants separately, allowing acclimatisation and protecting plants against movement in transit. No competitor prose or photographs were copied.

## Rastlina facts

- Plant sets: plant, self-watering pot and soil mix, according to the About page and selected listing.
- Shipping policy: dispatch within three days; courier delivery typically five to nine days. Estimates are distinguished from guarantees.
- Replacement policy: claims within ten days of delivery, subject to eligibility and verification; retain packaging and label. Complete unboxing video is required for missing-item/empty-box claims.
- Delivery photos are illustrative; packaging materials and layout may vary by order.
